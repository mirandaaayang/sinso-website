From a small Ningbo print shop in 1998 to a globally recognized packaging manufacturer — our story is one of continuous investment and innovation.

Our Story 我们的故事

Ningbo's Leading Color Packaging Manufacturer

华东地区彩印包装领跑者

Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. was established in 1998 in Fenghua, Ningbo, Zhejiang Province. Over 25 years we have grown from a regional print shop into a nationally recognized high-tech enterprise, operating over 12,000 square meters of modern production space.

宁波新兆印业有限公司成立于1998年，位于浙江省宁波市奉化区。25年间，我们从一家区域印刷厂成长为国家认定的高新技术企业，拥有超过12,000平方米的现代化生产空间。

Today we are the designated packaging and printing supplier for Walmart, Target, Disney, Home Depot, Best Buy, Lowe's, Petco, Walgreens, Academy, and Wilko — brands that demand the highest standards of color accuracy, structural integrity, and compliance.

如今，我们是沃尔玛、塔吉特、迪士尼、家得宝、百思买、Lowe's、Petco、Walgreens、Academy及Wilko的指定包装印刷供应商——这些品牌对色彩精准度、结构完整性和合规性要求最为严苛。

Our History 发展历程

25 Years of Growth

25年发展历程

2024

Patents, New Equipment & Pioneer Awards

专利、新设备及先锋奖项

Approved for 1 invention patent and 1 utility model patent. Added high-speed slitting, die-cutting, and carton gluing machines. Awarded Pioneer Enterprise of New Productive Forces and Specialized Special & New SME. Passed continuous cleaner production review.

成功获批1项发明专利及1项实用新型专利。新增高速分切机、全自动模切机和高速糊盒机。荣获新质生产力先锋企业及专精特新中小企业称号。通过持续清洁生产评审。

2023

G7 Certification & Digital Transformation

G7认证及数字化转型

Passed G7 color certification (ISO 12647-2). Awarded Green Factory, 5-Star Enterprise Management, and Municipal Innovation Pioneer. Established provincial high-tech R&D center and digital workshop. Added Heidelberg XL105 8+1 press. MOM system obtained national software copyright.

通过G7色彩认证（ISO 12647-2）。获评绿色工厂、五星级企业管理奖及市级创新先锋。成立省级高新技术企业研究中心和数字化车间。新增海德堡XL105 8+1印刷机。MOM系统获国家版权局软件著作权。

2022

RGB Ultra-Wide Color Gamut Printing

RGB超广色域印刷

Introduced Heidelberg XL75 8+1 eight-color press, breaking into ultra-wide color gamut offset printing technology.

引进海德堡XL75 8+1八色机，突破传统印刷，踏入超广色域胶印新技术领域。

2021

National High-Tech Enterprise

国家高新技术企业

Recognized as a National High-tech Enterprise. Independently developed APS+MES production management system driving company-wide operations.

获评国家高新技术企业。自主研发APS+MES系统，驱动全公司生产管理。

2015–2020

East China Market Leader

华东地区彩印包装领跑者

Introduced spot color automatic matching system. Added Heidelberg CD74-7 seven-color press. Became the designated supplier for Disney, Academy, and Home Depot through innovation-driven lean manufacturing.

引进专色油墨自动配色系统。购入海德堡CD74-7七色机。通过创新管理和精益生产，成为迪士尼、Academy及家得宝指定供应商。

2011–2014

GMI Certification & Major US Retailers

GMI认证及进入美国主流零售商

Introduced Heidelberg XL-75 and XL-105 presses. Adopted world-leading color management. Passed GMI certification. Became designated supplier for Target, Walmart, Best Buy, Lowe's, Petco, Wilko, and Walgreens.

引进海德堡XL-75及XL-105印刷机。采用国际最前端色彩管理系统。通过GMI认证。成为塔吉特、沃尔玛、百思买、Lowe's、Petco、Wilko及Walgreens指定供应商。

2001–2010

First Global Partnerships

首个全球合作伙伴关系

Introduced Japanese Kodak CTP system and Heidelberg CD102 five-color press. Began partnerships with Walmart and Target.

引进日本柯达CTP制版系统和海德堡CD102五色机。开始与沃尔玛和塔吉特建立合作关系。
