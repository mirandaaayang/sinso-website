From a small Ningbo print shop in 1998 to a G7-certified global packaging manufacturer — 25 years of continuous investment, innovation, and trust.

Our Story 我们的故事

Ningbo's Leading Color Packaging Manufacturer

华东地区彩印包装领跑者

Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. was established in 1998 in Fenghua, Ningbo, Zhejiang Province. Over 25 years we have grown from a regional print shop into a nationally recognized high-tech enterprise, operating over 12,000 square meters of modern production space.

宁波新兆印业有限公司成立于1998年，位于浙江省宁波市奉化区。25年间，我们从一家区域印刷厂成长为国家认定的高新技术企业，拥有超过12,000平方米的现代化生产空间。

Today we are the designated packaging and printing supplier for Walmart, Target, Disney, Home Depot, Best Buy, Lowe's, Petco, Walgreens, and Wilko — brands that demand the highest standards of color accuracy, structural integrity, and compliance.

如今，我们是沃尔玛、塔吉特、迪士尼、家得宝、百思买、Lowe's、Petco、Walgreens及Wilko的指定包装印刷供应商——这些品牌对色彩精准度、结构完整性和合规性要求最为严苛。
