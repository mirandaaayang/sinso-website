Common Questions

常见问题

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
最小起订量（MOQ）是多少？
MOQ varies by product type. Generally 1,000 units for folding cartons and paper cards, and 5,000 units for RFID labels. Contact us for your specific requirements.
最小起订量因产品类型而异。折叠纸盒和纸卡通常为1,000件，RFID标签为5,000件。请联系我们了解您的具体需求。
What are your standard lead times?
标准交货期是多久？
Standard production runs take 15–25 business days after artwork approval. Rush orders may be accommodated — please discuss your timeline with our sales team.
标准生产周期在图稿确认后为15–25个工作日。可安排加急订单——请与我们的销售团队讨论您的时间要求。
What trade terms do you offer?
提供哪些贸易条款？
We ship primarily FOB Ningbo. CIF, EXW, and DDP terms can be arranged. We handle all export documentation.
我们主要按宁波离岸价（FOB）发货。可安排成本加保险加运费（CIF）、工厂交货（EXW）及完税后交货（DDP）条款。我们负责所有出口文件。
Can I get physical samples before placing an order?
下订单前可以获得实物样品吗？
Yes. We offer pre-production samples (with sample fee, refundable on order) and can send existing samples from our portfolio. Check the sample request box in the form above.
可以。我们提供生产前样品（收取样品费，下订单后退还），也可发送我们现有产品组合中的样品。请勾选上方表单中的样品申请选项。
Are you compliant with US retail vendor requirements?
是否符合美国零售商供应商要求？
Yes. We are a certified supplier to Walmart, Target, Disney, Home Depot, Best Buy, and others. Our team is familiar with their vendor guides, testing requirements, and packaging compliance standards.
是的。我们是沃尔玛、塔吉特、迪士尼、家得宝、百思买等品牌的认证供应商。我们的团队熟悉其供应商指南、测试要求和包装合规标准。
Do you provide design and structural engineering support?
是否提供设计和结构工程支持？
Yes. Our in-house design team can help with dieline creation, 3D mockups, structural engineering, and print-ready file setup at no additional charge for active orders.
是的。我们的内部设计团队可协助制作刀版、3D效果图、结构工程及印前文件设置，活跃订单无需额外收费。
